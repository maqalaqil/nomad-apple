A library that travels with you

A small collection can be more useful than a large one you have never opened. Start with a book you enjoy, a reference you use often, and notes that belong to you.

Before you disconnect

1. Download or import the files you want to keep.
2. Open each file and check the pages or chapters you need.
3. Prepare a compatible AI model if you plan to use local inference.
4. Test the workflows you need with networking turned off.
5. Keep a separate backup of your own work.

A place for your ideas

Save a question while you read. Add a note when you discover something useful. Bring your documents together around a project, a place, or a subject you want to learn.
