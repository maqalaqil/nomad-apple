## Binary release gates

1. The [native source inventory](NATIVE_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md) now identifies the exact Kiwix release recipe, supporting dependencies, 19 verified source/build archives, and bundled notices. A source rebuild comparison and publication alongside a binary remain separate steps.
2. Provide applicable notices and corresponding source, including build instructions and modifications. A URL to this Apache-licensed repository alone is not a complete GPL source-compliance plan for the statically linked executable.
3. Resolve App Store terms against the GPL components, obtaining applicable additional permission or choosing replacement components if necessary. Publishing source does not by itself resolve restrictions on binary distribution. This audit has not established an App Store exception for the pinned dependencies; it does not claim that all GPL apps are categorically barred. See the [GPLv3 distribution conditions](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html) and [Apple's standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).
4. Verify that the MathJax adapter source/provenance checks pass and review every generated npm/font/asset notice. Do not infer a license from a package being publicly downloadable or used by another project.
5. Audit content separately. Catalog entries are download references, not grants of redistribution rights. Preserve Wikipedia attribution, OpenStreetMap/map-data terms, course licenses, and individual model licenses; do not bundle user files or test downloads into a release.

No third-party license is changed by this document. See [App Store readiness](APP_STORE_READINESS.md) for the remaining packaging and validation work.

## Existing Kiwix Apple distribution

The official [Kiwix Apple source](https://github.com/kiwix/kiwix-apple) is GPL-3.0 and links its iOS/macOS App Store releases. The [branded-app project](https://github.com/kiwix/kiwix-apple-branded) also documents external publisher accounts. This is relevant distribution precedent, not an automatically inherited exception or evidence that NOMAD has supplied its complete corresponding source. The combined executable licensing, exact native dependency inventory/build sources, notices and applicable Store terms still need a documented publisher decision before NOMAD binary distribution. Paid enrollment does not settle those obligations.

The source now adds **Content credits** accessible from Offline help, Content catalog, and the map toolbar. It supplies a readable offline OSM copyright URL, separates dataset terms from software licenses, and displays the exact bundled Qwen license offline. This addresses a concrete presentation gap: the map renderer sanitizes archive attribution to plain text, losing link destinations. The renderer continues to reject remote navigation; no untrusted attribution HTML is enabled. These changes are included in **verified 1.0 (10) archives and distribution packages**. The uploaded 1.0 (9) builds remain unchanged. Archive/export verification is not a public release or a content-rights declaration; see [current release status](APP_STORE_READINESS.md).

Apple's [Content Rights field](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information/) covers third-party material the app contains, shows or accesses. [Guideline 5.2.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#intellectual-property) also addresses access under service terms. This evidence establishes specific grants and conditions, not a universal rights certification. The Content Rights declaration remains unsaved while the unresolved distribution conditions and actual archive notices are assessed. It must not be answered “no third-party content” merely because large datasets download separately.

For software, preserve the distinct issues: GPLv3 sections 5/6 address conveying a combined work and corresponding source; section 10 prohibits further restrictions. The current [Apple standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/) includes an open-source exception in its copying/modification clause but also a separate transfer/redistribution restriction. The exception alone is insufficient evidence to conclude that every GPL obligation is satisfied. No new EULA, waiver or third-party license exception has been accepted or invented by this work.
